# devInstagram (Trusted) - CLAUDE.md

Not code. This folder is the "Trusted" sales methodology: Nino's sales approach built on belief, with saved Instagram sales tips tested against it. The name is confusing. The Instagram tool itself is `~/devUtils/devInstagram` (command `instagrame`). This folder holds what was made from its output.

## The files

- `Instagram_sales_catalogue.csv` - 51 saved sales posts, one row each. Columns: Post, Author, Stage, Source it draws on, Verdict, What to keep, What fails / why. Verdict is Keep, Adapt, Drop or Off topic. The Post value matches the file names under `~/devUtils/devInstagram/data/posts/` (for example `SALES-1-start-here-...`), where each post's extracted JSON and PowerPoint live.
- `Trusted_sales_methodology_starter.pptx` - 16 slides. The method and its argument: the core belief, the trust equation (Maister, Green and Galford), what is borrowed and from whom, the five stages, the integrity test, how the Instagram tips were sorted, what failed, what Instagram cannot teach, next steps. Draft for discussion.
- `Trusted_sales_playbook.pptx` - 15 slides. The practical version: each of the five stages with what to do, qualifying, objections, big accounts, lines we don't use, ten things to carry, sources.

## The method in brief

People buy from people they believe. Belief is earned by telling the truth when it costs you. Five stages, each answering the buyer's real question: Earn the right ("Can I trust you?"), Find the gap ("Do you understand me?"), Teach ("What am I missing?"), Make change safe ("Is it safe to move?"), Keep your word ("Did you do what you said?"). Every tactic must pass the integrity test: would I be happy if the buyer saw exactly what I am doing, and why?

## Rules for work here

- Credit the source. Take the principle. Do not reskin someone else's method.
- A new tip goes through the integrity test and gets a row in the catalogue with a verdict and a reason before it goes near a deck.
- Keep the catalogue and the decks in step: the counts on the "What's in the collection" slide come from the Verdict column.
- To pull in new posts, use `instagrame` in `~/devUtils/devInstagram`, not this folder.
- Style: short sentences, plain words, hyphens not em dashes.
